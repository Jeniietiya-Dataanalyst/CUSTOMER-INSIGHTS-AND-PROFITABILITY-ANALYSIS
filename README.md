# 📊 Customer Insights & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales, customer behavior, and profitability trends using **Power BI**. The goal was to provide actionable business insights by identifying high-performing segments, uncovering regional disparities, and highlighting growth opportunities.

The dashboard consolidates data from **2015 to 2017**, enabling management to track revenue drivers, profit margins, and customer demographics.

---

## 🛠 Methodology

The following steps were undertaken to prepare, model, and visualize the dataset in Power BI:

### 1. **Data Loading & Preparation**

* Imported raw sales data for 2015, 2016, and 2017.
* Checked for data quality issues, including duplicates and missing values.
* Standardized inconsistent entries and adjusted column data types.

### 2. **Data Transformation**

* Merged relevant columns for improved readability.
* Appended the three yearly sales datasets into a single consolidated table.
* Performed table merges where necessary to enrich the dataset.
* Created new calculated columns and KPIs for advanced analysis.

### 3. **Data Modeling**

* Built relationships between tables in **Model View** (one-to-many).
* Created calculated measures using **DAX functions** (e.g., profit margin, customer averages).

### 4. **Dashboard Development**

* Designed an interactive **Customer & Profitability Insights Dashboard**.
* Visualized key metrics such as revenue by region, gender, occupation, and product category.
* Incorporated a **time-series sales trend** to track performance across years.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* **Revenue & Profitability**

  * Total Revenue: **\$24.91M** | Total Profit: **\$10.46M** | Profit Margin: **41.97%**
  * 56K+ transactions, 17.4K customers → avg. revenue ≈ **\$1,430 per customer**.

* **Customer Segmentation**

  * **Professionals** generated the highest revenue (**\$8.5M**).
  * Balanced gender split: Male (**50.23%**) vs Female (**49.14%**).

* **Regional Analysis**

  * **Australia (\$7.4M)** was the top-performing region.
  * Weaker markets include **Canada (\$1.8M)** and **France (\$2.4M)**.

* **Product Category**

  * **Clothing dominates (95% of total revenue)**, while Bikes & Accessories contribute marginally.

* **Sales Trend**

  * Strong growth from **2015 (\$6.4M) → 2016 (\$9.3M)**.
  * Slight decline in **2017 (\$9.2M)** — potential early warning of market stagnation.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Diversify Products** → Reduce over-reliance on Clothing by boosting Bikes & Accessories.
2. **Expand Successful Regional Strategies** → Apply Australia’s growth model to weaker regions.
3. **Target New Segments** → Develop strategies to engage clerical/manual workers.
4. **Investigate 2017 Decline** → Explore competition, product fatigue, or customer churn causes.
5. **Set Benchmarks** → Use 2016 as a performance baseline to exceed in future years.
